What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a directory of product links organised to make shopping research easier. Use it to discover listings and build a shortlist, then verify the seller, selected variant, measurements and full cost. A directory entry is a starting point, not a quality guarantee or an authenticity certificate.

How to use a Kakobuy spreadsheet

Use the spreadsheet to discover options, then make the buying decision on the live listing.

Start with a category and set a maximum delivered budget. Save two or three relevant links rather than collecting dozens of similar products. For each option, record the seller, variant, stated measurements, item price and unresolved questions.

A directory may describe an item more broadly than the seller does. Check that the link still opens the intended product and that the selected colour and size match your notes. Avoid paying while essential details remain unclear.

Before proceeding: confirm the destination page, selected variant and total-cost assumptions.

The year in a spreadsheet title is not evidence that every entry has been recently checked.

For a Kakobuy Spreadsheet 2026 shortlist, freshness means verifying each live product link, current variant availability and the terms presented before payment. An older review can still be informative, but it may describe a different seller batch or product specification.

Research / Kakobuy links03

How to check Kakobuy links before ordering

A working link still needs a product and seller check.

Open the destination and compare its title, photos, seller identity and selectable variants with the directory entry. A listing can change while retaining the same URL. If the page displays a different item, stop and search again rather than assuming the original description remains valid.

Record the exact variant and inspect the payment destination. Do not enter credentials into an unfamiliar page merely because a product directory linked to it. If a listing is unavailable, treat an alternative as a new purchase requiring its own checks.

Link checklist: intended domain, matching product, identifiable seller and clear checkout terms.

Quality control / Photos04

Kakobuy QC photos: what to inspect first

Check order accuracy before judging small cosmetic details.

Begin with item count, colour, size label and selected variant. Then inspect seams, alignment, obvious marks and functional parts such as zips or buckles where photographs allow. Compare views under similar lighting; shadows and white balance can alter how colour appears.

QC photographs show visible features at a particular moment. They do not establish authenticity, skin safety, waterproof performance or durability. If a measurement or angle is missing, request it while the applicable inspection or return window is still open.

Inspect in order: correct item → correct variant → measurements → visible condition.

Quality control / Requests05

How to request useful QC measurements

Ask for a specific view or measurement that could change your decision.

“Please check quality” gives little direction. A better request names the item, the area of concern and the evidence needed: for example, a flat chest-width measurement with the tape ends visible, or a close view of a zip fastening.

Separate a visible defect from a preference. An uneven seam can be photographed; whether a fit feels relaxed depends on your reference measurements. Confirm whether extra images or handling incur fees before requesting them, and check the provider's response and inspection deadlines.

Example request: “Please photograph the garment flat and show the chest width from underarm to underarm.”

Budget / Shipping estimate06

How to build a Kakobuy shipping estimate

Estimate delivery using the packed parcel, not item price alone.

Shipping quotations may depend on destination, actual weight, dimensions, service level and product restrictions. Some services use volumetric weight when a parcel occupies more space than its scale weight suggests. Use the exact carrier's published calculation rather than assuming a universal divisor.

Before packing, work with a range. After packing, replace assumptions with confirmed measurements and readjust your budget. Check handling, protective packaging, optional cover and any other quoted fees separately. A pre-packing estimate is not a guaranteed final charge.

Required inputs: destination, packed dimensions, actual weight, eligible contents and live service terms.

Delivery / Service choice07

Choosing a Kakobuy shipping line for the UK

Choose an eligible service that fits your budget and timing; there is no universal best line.

Compare live quotations for the same parcel. Look at tracking coverage, estimated transit range, item exclusions, compensation limits and how claims are submitted. A cheap headline rate can be less useful if your contents are excluded or compensation would not cover the loss.

Allow for processing, collection and customs time in addition to transit estimates. For a birthday, holiday or event, build in a margin and avoid treating an estimate as a promise. Confirm the final postcode is served before paying.

Decision rule: eligibility first, then full price, delivery range and written cover terms.

United Kingdom / Preparation08

Kakobuy UK buying checklist

Check destination-specific import guidance and calculate what you will pay in pounds.

Confirm your delivery region, postcode and contact details. Great Britain and Northern Ireland can have different import arrangements, so use the official guidance that matches the actual destination. Do not assume a forum answer applies to your parcel or circumstances.

Read GOV.UK guidance on goods sent from abroad before ordering and check the carrier's handling terms. This guide does not set tax rates or thresholds. Use accurate item descriptions and values, and confirm product restrictions through official sources.

Budget check: payable exchange rate, applicable import charges, carrier fees and a contingency.

Category / Shoes09

Kakobuy shoes: compare measurements before sizes

A UK, EU or US size conversion is a starting point, not a fit guarantee.

Measure both feet using the seller's stated method and compare the larger measurement with the exact listing's size chart. Consider width, intended socks and the shoe's shape. Do not substitute outsole length for usable internal length; they answer different questions.

If QC measurements are available, ask what was measured and how. An insole measurement may be useful, but it does not fully describe toe-box height or width. Also check whether keeping the shoe box changes parcel dimensions and protection.

Before checkout: model-specific chart, measurement method, width needs and return terms.

Category / Knitwear10

Kakobuy hoodies and sweaters: build a fit reference

Compare the listing with a similar garment you already like wearing.

Lay your reference hoodie flat and record chest width, body length and sleeve length using consistent endpoints. Compare those figures with the seller's chart, allowing for any stated manufacturing tolerance. A size marked “L” can have very different measurements across listings.

For knitwear, look for care instructions and stated fibre composition. Photographs cannot reliably establish warmth, softness or shrinkage. If the shoulder construction differs from your reference garment, a sleeve measurement may require a different interpretation.

Fit note: record whether each figure is a flat width or a full circumference.

Category / Everyday basics11

Kakobuy T-shirts: check cut, fabric and print

Evaluate the fit and material description separately from the product photograph.

Check chest width, length, shoulder position and sleeve opening. A styled photograph may use a larger size or a model with different proportions. “Oversized” does not provide a measurable fit standard.

Read the stated fabric composition and care guidance. If fabric weight is supplied, use it as one comparison point rather than proof of quality. For printed shirts, inspect placement and obvious surface defects in available QC images, while recognising that wash durability cannot be confirmed from a photograph.

Shortlist fields: cut, measurements, declared composition, print details and care instructions.

Category / Outerwear12

Kakobuy jackets: check layers and parcel size

Choose jacket measurements for the layers you intend to wear underneath.

Compare against a jacket with a similar use, then review chest, sleeve and body length. Check the listing for lining, insulation, closures and pocket details. Marketing terms such as “technical” are not a substitute for documented performance information.

Bulky outerwear may create a larger parcel even when relatively light. Ask for a shipping estimate that considers packed dimensions. Treat waterproof, protective or safety claims as requiring appropriate evidence; ordinary QC photographs cannot verify those properties.

Practical check: layering room, closure function, stated care and packing requirements.

Category / Bottoms13

Kakobuy trousers and shorts: measure beyond the waist

Waist size alone cannot tell you how trousers will fit.

Compare waist, rise, hip, thigh, inseam and leg opening where those figures are available. Use a similar pair you own as a reference. For elastic waists, distinguish relaxed measurements from stretched measurements and ask which the chart shows.

A hypothetical example: a pair may match your waist but have a shorter rise and narrower thigh than your reference. Choosing it solely by waist could therefore disappoint. If a crucial measurement is missing, request it or remove the item from your shortlist.

Minimum fit record: waist method, rise, thigh room and desired length.

Category / Coordinated pieces14

Kakobuy jerseys and sets: verify every component

Check exactly what the selected price includes before ordering a set.

A listing image can show several pieces while the chosen variant includes only one. Read the option label and confirm whether the top, shorts, trousers or accessories are included. Check top and bottom measurements independently because the same size label may not suit both.

For jerseys, compare cut, sleeve dimensions and any selected personalisation. Ask how customisation affects cancellation or returns before submitting a name or number. QC images can reveal spelling and placement issues, but do not prove licensing or authenticity.

Order check: component count, separate measurements, selected design and customisation text.

Category / Daily carry15

Kakobuy bags, belts and accessories: check practical fit

Start with the dimensions and function you need, then consider appearance.

For bags, compare the opening and usable internal space with the objects you carry. External dimensions may not tell you whether a laptop fits through the opening. Inspect handles, strap attachment points, lining and closures where visible.

For belts, confirm whether the advertised length includes the buckle and where the usable holes sit. For headwear, check circumference and adjustment range. For underwear, confirm sizing and hygiene-related return terms before ordering; do not assume ordinary clothing return conditions apply.

Function checklist: usable dimensions, adjustment, fastenings, declared materials and category-specific terms.

Restrictions / Fragrances16

Kakobuy perfumes: verify shipping before purchase

Do not assume a fragrance can travel on the same service as ordinary clothing.

Fragrances and other liquids may require special acceptance, packaging or documentation. Check the exact product, volume, destination and proposed service with the provider before buying. A category link or successful domestic delivery does not establish international shipping eligibility.

Read the relevant carrier's current prohibited and restricted goods information. Obtain written confirmation when terms are unclear. Do not conceal contents or change descriptions to bypass restrictions. If no suitable service is confirmed, remove the item from your parcel plan.

Proceed only with: confirmed acceptance, required packaging and a clear total quote.

Planning / Kakobuy haul17

How to plan a manageable Kakobuy haul

Build a parcel around genuine needs and a delivered-cost ceiling.

Group your shortlist by fit certainty, shipping eligibility and expected parcel bulk. Remove items with unanswered sizing questions or incompatible shipping requirements before paying. Consolidation can change costs, but a larger parcel is not automatically the best value.

For a first purchase, a smaller order may make it easier to assess the process while limiting the amount committed. Keep an itemised record of costs and inspection status. Set aside contingency funds rather than spending the full budget on the advertised product prices.

Parcel plan: necessary items, compatible contents, realistic budget and time margin.

Trust / Due diligence18

Is Kakobuy legit? Questions to check before paying

This directory does not certify a platform or seller; evaluate current evidence before committing money.

Look for clear business and contact information, written fees, accessible terms and a documented complaint process. Read recent independent experiences from more than one source. Check whether reviews identify the order context and disclose incentives.

Separate platform service, individual seller performance and product authenticity: evidence about one does not settle the others. Understand the payment method's dispute conditions and time limits. Avoid interpreting a polished interface, a directory inclusion or a single successful order as a guarantee.

Unanswered essentials? Pause until the fees, responsibilities and available remedies are clear.

After ordering / Evidence19

Kakobuy returns and order issues: keep a clear record

Check the applicable terms and act promptly when an issue appears.

Before payment, save the listing, selected variant, size chart and displayed return conditions. After ordering, retain the order reference, payment record and relevant messages. A compact record makes it easier to explain what was ordered and how the delivered item differs.

If QC reveals a problem, identify it precisely and request a remedy through the provider's official process. Confirm deadlines, return delivery costs and any non-refundable fees. Do not assume a seller return, a platform complaint and a payment dispute follow the same rules.

Issue report: order reference, expected specification, clear evidence and requested resolution.

Decision / Shortlist20

How to compare Kakobuy spreadsheet finds

Compare equivalent variants using evidence and estimated delivered cost.

Create one row per exact product option. Record item price, measurements, declared materials, available QC evidence, return terms and delivery assumptions. Mark missing information as unknown rather than filling gaps with optimistic guesses.

Illustrative comparison: a £20 top with confirmed measurements may be a more useful option than a £16 top with no size chart, depending on your needs and total costs. Neither price proves better quality. Rank options by fit confidence, usable evidence and affordability, then recheck the chosen listing before payment.

Final decision: choose only when the important unknowns are acceptable to you.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product-link directory used to organise shopping options. It can help you discover listings, but it does not guarantee availability, authenticity, quality or delivery.

Is this an official Kakobuy website?

No. This is an independent directory and educational guide. It is not affiliated with Kakobuy, Weidian or Tmall and does not sell, ship or provide order support.

Does a spreadsheet price include shipping?

Do not assume so. Check whether the listed amount includes domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, applicable taxes and payment conversion charges.

Can QC photos prove authenticity?

No. QC photos can help assess visible condition, measurements and order accuracy. They cannot establish authenticity, material safety or long-term durability.

Which shipping line is best for UK orders?

There is no universal best line. Compare live options for your destination, parcel size, item restrictions, tracking, cover and total price before paying.

Are perfume and battery-powered items suitable for every shipping line?

No. Liquids, fragrances and batteries may face carrier restrictions. Confirm written acceptance for the exact item and destination before purchasing.
